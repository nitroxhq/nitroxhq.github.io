what even is nitrox?

real talk: somewhere along the way, technology stopped feeling as fun as it should. so much of the software we use every day feels unnecessarily restrictive, disconnected, or built around someone else's idea of how we should work. and there's so much more we could be doing with it!

i started nitrox because i wanted to explore what happens when we question those assumptions instead of accepting them. what if our tools were more expressive, more interconnected, more personal? what if great design wasn't just about looking good, but about making complicated things feel natural? and what if the software we rely on actually gave us more freedom?

that's what i'm trying to figure out with nitrox. i'm building tools, experimenting with ideas, and exploring better ways to create, learn, and interact with technology. it's early, and there's a lot left to figure out — but that's the fun part.

nitrox is a work in progress, and so is the vision. i'm building in the open, learning as i go, and trying to make things that should exist but don't (yet). if that sounds like your kind of thing, stick around. i'd love you to join me in any way you can.

- ellis,
founder of nitrox
