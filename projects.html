a better kind of technology.
built around you.

nitrox is exploring what software and hardware could be if we started with people, not platforms. tools that work together instead of trapping you in disconnected ecosystems. interfaces that adapt to you, instead of the other way around.

from organizing your thoughts to creating something entirely new, we're building toward a world where technology feels less like something you have to manage and more like an extension of what you want to do.

our guiding principle is simple: the application is the interface to your data, not the owner of your data. your work should remain yours — portable, interconnected, and accessible on your terms.

the core suite

the tools we use to think, work, explore, and connect with the world. separate experiences, designed to work better together.

tesseract — your productivity supertool. bring notes, tasks, projects, events, and ideas into one interconnected environment, without sacrificing flexibility or control.

prism — content consumption, on your terms. bring reading, RSS, saved content, and the feeds you care about together, without letting endless scrolling dictate your attention.

niko — an AI coworker for thinking, creating, and getting things done. a collaborative assistant that helps you explore ideas, untangle your workload, and turn intentions into action.

photon — a browser that puts you back in control of the web, with thoughtful tab management, broad extension support, and quality-of-life improvements that make browsing feel like yours.

airwaves — your music, all in one place. a seamless way to manage and enjoy your local and remote music libraries without splitting your listening experience across disconnected apps.

the creative suite

creative software should get out of your way without limiting what you can make. we're exploring powerful, approachable tools for the entire creative process — from the first idea to the finished work.

orchid — a modern design environment for interfaces, graphics, and visual ideas, with the flexibility of professional design software and the freedom of local-first workflows.

blueberry — a music production environment that brings approachable creation together with the depth and flexibility serious projects demand.

cobalt — a code editor that grows with you, from a fast, lightweight writing environment to a fully equipped development workspace.

more creative tools — we're also exploring dedicated tools for illustration, vector graphics, digital painting, pixel art, and animation. different crafts deserve great tools of their own.

systems & utilities

your devices should feel like yours, too. we're interested in making operating systems more customizable, software easier to access, and everyday computing less restrictive.

nexOS — an exploration of a more open computing experience: bringing useful software, deeper customization, and new life to devices across operating systems.

cosmetica — make your desktop your own. a vision for bringing customization, docks, panels, widgets, and personal expression to more computing environments.

the nitrox hardware vision — eventually, we'd love to explore devices of our own, from personal computers to mobile and audio hardware. technology shouldn't stop being thoughtfully designed when it leaves the screen.

still exploring.

not everything here exists yet, and that's okay. these are ideas we're developing, directions we're exploring, and problems we want to solve.

we're building toward something bigger, one project at a time. stay tuned.
