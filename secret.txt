            <h2>the creative suite</h2>
            <p>creative software should get out of your way without limiting what you can make. we're exploring powerful, approachable tools for the entire creative process — from the first idea to the finished work.</p>
        
            <p><b>orchid</b> — a modern design environment for interfaces, graphics, and visual ideas, with the flexibility of professional design software and the freedom of local-first workflows.</p>
        
            <p><b>blueberry</b> — a music production environment that brings approachable creation together with the depth and flexibility serious projects demand.</p>
        
            <p><b>cobalt</b> — a code editor that grows with you, from a fast, lightweight writing environment to a fully equipped development workspace.</p>
        
            <p><b>more creative tools</b> — we're also exploring dedicated tools for illustration, vector graphics, digital painting, pixel art, and animation. different crafts deserve great tools of their own.</p>
        </section>

            <h2>systems & utilities</h2>
            <p>your devices should feel like yours, too. we're interested in making operating systems more customizable, software easier to access, and everyday computing less restrictive.</p>
        
            <p><b>nexOS</b> — an exploration of a more open computing experience: bringing useful software, deeper customization, and new life to devices across operating systems.</p>
        
            <p><b>cosmetica</b> — make your desktop your own. a vision for bringing customization, docks, panels, widgets, and personal expression to more computing environments.</p>
        
            <p><b>the nitrox hardware vision</b> — eventually, we'd love to explore devices of our own, from personal computers to mobile and audio hardware. technology shouldn't stop being thoughtfully designed when it leaves the screen.</p>
